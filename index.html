A Structured Scientific Publication Dataset

Scientific publications generate enormous amounts of valuable information. Our dataset brings together structured metadata from historical Nature.com / Springer Nature publication records, making it easier for researchers, institutions, developers, and analytical teams to explore and process scholarly publication information.

Publication Information

Paper titles, DOIs, article URLs, publication dates, article types, journal information.

Author Intelligence

Author names, co-authorship, affiliations and institutional associations.

Geographic Information

Author country information, institutional locations where available.

Research Discovery

Research keywords, publication classification, topic exploration.

Built for Research, Analytics, and Data-Driven Innovation

Academic Research

Study publication trends and history.
